Anvil Office — How to use it

Made for Anvil Mobile Machining (portfolio demo — this is the same guide handed to the real client, with their name swapped out). One file, no passwords, no monthly fees, no internet needed. Your information stays on your computer.

Opening it

Double-click Anvil-Office.html. It opens in your web browser like a website, but it is not on the internet — it is yours. Bookmark it or pin it to your taskbar.

Important: always open it from the same place (same file, same computer), or your saved work won't show up. If you need to move it, make a backup first (see below) and restore it after.

Write an invoice (or estimate)

  1. Hit NEW INVOICE / ESTIMATE on the Home screen.
  2. Pick the customer. The invoice number fills itself in (your usual numbering).
  3. Type what the customer said the problem was, and where the work happens. The buttons under the problem box drop in their machine's serial number for you.
  4. Add a line for each thing you did (these print as the numbered task list).
  5. Add the charges — put in hours and rate and it does the math.
  6. Hit SAVE & PRINT. In the print window choose Save as PDF to email it, or print it on paper. It looks just like your old invoices.

Start it as an Estimate and it prints with the word "Estimate" on it. When the customer says go, open it and switch it to Invoiced — same paper, now it says Invoice.

When a check comes in

  1. Open JOBS & INVOICES, tap the invoice.
  2. Hit RECORD PAYMENT, put in the amount. Paid in full marks it PAID by itself.

The Home screen always shows who owes you and how many days it's been. Red means past due.

Receipts

Bought welding gas, parts, tools? Hit ADD A RECEIPT, type who, how much, done. It sorts itself into the right category for taxes.

Once a month — statements

  1. Download your bank and credit-card statements as CSV (same downloads you already do).
  2. Hit IMPORT BANK / CARD CSV and pick the file.
  3. It shows you every line first. Card payments, transfers, and things you already brought in are unchecked automatically. Uncheck anything personal, then hit Import.

Tax time

On the Money screen hit TAX EXPORT. It makes one spreadsheet file with the year's business income and expenses by category. Hand that to your tax person. Done.

Back it up — once a week

Go to SETTINGS, hit BACK UP NOW. It saves one file (Anvil-Backup-date.json) into your Downloads. Keep it on a USB stick or email it to yourself.

New computer, or something went wrong? Open Anvil Office there, go to Settings, hit RESTORE FROM BACKUP, pick your newest backup file. Everything comes back.
